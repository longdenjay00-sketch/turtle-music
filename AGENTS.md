## 2. Prime Directive: Bioacoustic & Vibrational Curriculum Standards

When authoring or expanding lessons and observations in this vault:
1. **Zero Fluff:** Omit filler. Open directly with YAML frontmatter and structured markdown headers.
2. **Bridge Physics, Bioacoustics & Vibrational Theory:**
   - Distinguish clearly between **established acoustic physics / chelonian neurobiology** (e.g., tympanic-columellar middle ear resonance `100 Hz – 700 Hz`, underwater acoustic impedance `Z = 1.48 × 10^6 Pa·s/m`, carapace/plastron bone conduction, autonomic parasympathetic vagal tone) and **holistic sound healing / tuning traditions** (e.g., `432 Hz` Verdi tuning vs. `440 Hz` ISO 16, Solfeggio intervals, cymatic nodal geometry, rhythmic entrainment). Analyze both with rigorous mathematical precision (`Hz`, `dB SPL`, `m/s`, `Pa·s/m`, `BPM`, `ms`).
3. **Reproducible Computational Labs:** Every lesson must include executable `python3` or `bash` verification commands using `scripts/turtle_acoustics_lab.py` or standard `aarch64` Python libraries (`math`, `wave`, `struct`).
4. **Three-Tier Graded Problem Sets:** Every curriculum lesson under `lessons/` must conclude with three graded problems:
   - **Level 1 (Direct Execution):** Fundamental frequency, wavelength, harmonic ratio, or chelonian threshold calculation.
   - **Level 2 (Applied Scenario):** Multi-step acoustic impedance, air-to-water transmission loss, tempo-to-heart-rate entrainment, or instrument spectral overlap analysis.
   - **Level 3 (The Trap):** Real-world physical or biological pitfalls (e.g., confusing airborne `dB SPL re 20 μPa` with underwater `dB re 1 μPa`, placing a subwoofer directly on the aquarium stand causing stressful `60 Hz` structural resonance, or mistaking high-frequency bird-like trills `> 3000 Hz` for audible turtle stimuli).
5. **Explicit Units & Worked Solutions:** Every numerical figure must carry explicit physical units (`Hz`, `m/s`, `cm`, `dB SPL`, `Pa·s/m`, `BPM`, `°C`). Bare numbers are rejected.
6. **Obsidian Graph Interlinking:** Connect every lesson, repertoire note, and observation entry via bidirectional wikilinks (`[[path/to/note|Label]]`).
